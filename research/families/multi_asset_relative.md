# Cross-Asset, Relative-Value and Regime-Filter Strategies: Family Report (v2, web sweep)

Prepared 2026-10-03 for the Lucid Trading 50K LucidFlex project. All times are **ET**. "RTH" = 09:30-16:00 ET for ES/NQ (futures settle 16:15, cash close 16:00). Data assumed available: 1-minute OHLC (no volume) for S&P 500 (ES/MES proxy), Nasdaq 100 (NQ/MNQ proxy) and Gold (GC/MGC proxy), Nov 2010 to Sep 2026; daily futures and the VIX family from Yahoo (`^VIX`, `^VIX9D`, `^VIX3M`, `^VIX6M`, `^VVIX`, `DX-Y.NYB`, `^TNX`, `ES=F`, `NQ=F`, `GC=F`, `BTC-USD`). Crude is not available 2024-2026.

**Method.** This v2 is a full web sweep (about 30 WebSearch queries, 12 of them in extended mode, and about 40 page fetches) across Quantpedia, QuantConnect, SSRN/arXiv/JFE, CXO Advisory, Options Cafe, TradingView open-source scripts, GitHub, NexusFi/NinjaTrader forums, prop-firm education sites and independent backtests (Quantitativo, Trade That Swing, algotr, tradingstats, stsfutures). It keeps the canonical-source material from the earlier sweep (Hasbrouck, Huth-Abergel, Baur-Lucey, Moreira-Muir, Kritzman, Mesfin 2026) and adds: the full text of Baltussen-Da-Lammers-Martens (JFE 2021) with per-contract ES/NQ/GC coefficients; Della Corte-Kosowski-Wang overnight-intraday reversal with VIX conditioning; Options Cafe and thetrading.tools VIX/VIX3M event statistics through 2026; CXO's negative test of the Connors 5% VIX rule; Simon-Campasano/Quantpedia out-of-sample decay; the PLOS ONE VIX-term-structure walk-forward study; the TradingView "NY VIX Channel" implied-move day-trade; stsfutures 2011-2025 ES/NQ correlation and range statistics; aeromir GC/ES 5-minute correlation; the Mesfin VVG regime classifier; 2025 gold/equity correlation evidence; and several practitioner gold/DXY/VIX scripts. Where a canonical rule set is paywalled or bot-blocked (Quantified Strategies, Quantifiable Edges, ScienceDirect abstracts), the most standard objective interpretation is written down and marked as such.

Evidence quality scale: 1 = anecdote/vendor claim, 2 = single practitioner backtest or script, 3 = large-sample practitioner study or single working paper, 4 = peer-reviewed or multiple independent backtests, 5 = peer-reviewed with multiple independent out-of-sample confirmations.

**Data flags:** [VOL] needs volume; [VIX-INTRADAY] needs intraday VIX (we have daily only); [VIXF] needs VIX futures; [DXY-INTRADAY] needs intraday dollar index; [FUTURES] needs real futures prices (cash-index proxies distort it); [DAILY-ONLY] the signal is daily and is used as an intraday filter.

## 0. Summary

Scope: ES-vs-NQ spread/ratio mean reversion and lead-lag; SMT (ICT) cross-index divergence; relative-strength selection between ES/NQ/GC; cross-asset market intraday momentum (rest-of-day predicts last 30 minutes) with volatility conditioning; overnight-intraday (CO-OC) reversal conditioned on VIX changes; VIX level, VIX stretch and VIX term-structure gates; VIX implied-move channels; synthetic OHLC volatility (Williams VIX Fix); volatility-managed sizing; correlation-regime, turbulence and absorption-ratio kill switches; GMM/VVG regime gating; gold-vs-equities safe-haven and DXY-vs-gold filters; Bitcoin-as-leading-indicator; VIX futures basis (signal only).

Headline findings:

1. **The best-evidenced tradeable *entry* signal in this family is market intraday momentum across assets (Baltussen et al., JFE 2021, 60+ futures 1974-2020): the return from the prior close to 15:30 predicts the 15:30-16:00 return.** Per-contract: NQ beta_ROD = 6.36 (t = 7.97, OOS R^2 3.76%), ES beta_ROD = 6.18 (t = 4.97, OOS R^2 2.29%), GC beta_ROD = 1.09 (t ~ 2.9, OOS R^2 0.08%, i.e. weak). The equity-index timing portfolio earned 6.86%/yr at 3.96% vol (Sharpe 1.73, 55% daily hit rate) gross; "a positive net Sharpe ratio when we assume transaction cost equal to a tick" in ES. Gao-Han-Li-Zhou (JFE 2018) find the same for SPY (first half-hour predicts last half-hour; Sharpe 1.08; stronger on volatile, high-volume and macro-news days). This is the one cross-asset effect that is peer-reviewed, replicated, present in 2000-2020 as strongly as 1974-1999, codeable from OHLC, and naturally flat by the close.
2. **Filters and sizing beat signals everywhere else.** Volatility-managed sizing (Moreira-Muir 2017) remains the single most important rule for a $2,000 EOD-trailing account. VIX/VIX3M term-structure gating has consistent practitioner statistics through 2026: entering longs when the ratio crosses back below 1.0 after a backwardation episode gave +3.04% over 5 days with an 88% hit rate (43 episodes, Sep 2009-Jun 2026, Options Cafe) and +3.9%/quarter with 75% positive (103 episodes since 2010, thetrading.tools). By contrast the Connors/TradingMarkets "VIX 5% above its 10-day SMA" rule did **not** beat buy-and-hold in CXO's 1990-2007 test (196% vs 322%; mean 5-day return 0.25% after de-overlapping).
3. **ES/NQ lead-lag at 1-minute resolution is not exploitable** (Hasbrouck 2003: discovery for each index happens in its own E-mini; Huth-Abergel: a market-order laggard strategy cannot beat the spread; the only "profitable after costs" lead-lag paper, Int. J. Forecasting 2024, is DAX cross-listings at LOB level). ES/NQ **correlation is 0.927 on daily returns 2011-May 2025 and ~0.95 intraday**, rising to 0.94-0.99 in sell-offs, so ES is mostly a redundant copy of NQ for timing purposes, and SMT divergence has no published statistics at all.
4. **ES/NQ beta-neutral spread mean reversion** has thin, positive daily-horizon evidence (single GitHub notebook, Sharpe ~1.7 2021-2025) and none at the intraday horizon; the SPY/QQQ pair at PairTradingLab scored Sharpe 0.44 before the service shut down citing "relationship deterioration" and "multiple-testing bias". Only worth testing with a hard z-stop and a correlation gate.
5. **Gold is no longer a reliable intraday risk-off hedge.** GC/ES 5-minute RTH correlation is +0.17 over six years (aeromir); 1-month daily correlation hit +0.945 in September 2024 and gold was sold with equities on 7-8 April 2025 (margin liquidation) before making new highs. The peer-reviewed safe-haven effect (Baur-Lucey, Baur-McDermott) is real only at the extreme (bottom 1-5% of equity days) and short-lived; the ScienceDirect 2025 study "The diminishing lustre" finds gold positively correlated with stocks in most post-2005 crises. Gold-DXY inverse correlation (~ -0.7 long run) also weakened in 2023-2026 (both rose). Use gold/DXY only as day-type filters.
6. **Regime-gated 60-75 minute holds are the only intraday architecture that survived a 2021-2025 MNQ walk-forward** (Mesfin 2026: 14 OHLCV signal families fail a 2.0-pt friction floor; two GMM-regime-gated pullback entries pass with OOS T = 3.11 and 4.30). His VVG "high gap + high first-30-min move + high first-bar volume" classifier flags 4.4% of days which reverse from their intraday peak 77.6% of the time, but no directional rule survived because 2024 drifted up (+40.7 pts mean) while 2025 crashed (-42.5 pts).
7. **Prop fit.** This family's trade generators are: #8 (last-30-minute momentum, 1 trade/day, flat at 16:00), #9 (gap/CO-OC reversal on high-VIX-change days), #22 (regime-gated pullbacks), #1 (hedged spread, with stops) and #19 (gold shock-day long, 5-15 days/yr). Everything else is an overlay that decides *which* instrument, *which* direction, *how big* and *whether to trade at all*. The dangerous ones for a $2,000 EOD-trailing floor are unhedged laggard catch-up trades and spread trades without a z-stop.

## 1. ES-NQ beta-neutral spread z-score mean reversion (intraday)

- **Origin:** Classic pairs/stat-arb (Gatev-Goetzmann-Rouwenhorst 2006); futures implementations: QuantConnect ES/NQ pairs algorithms (minute data, cointegration test and pairs open/close every 1 hour, TP/SL check every minute; weights w_ES = 1/(1+beta), w_NQ = beta/(1+beta)); NexusFi "Statistical Arbitrage Systems for Futures" (spread = P_A - beta P_B, z entry +-1.5, exit at 0; half-life < 5 days = intraday/overnight candidate); GitHub CommunityPokeOrg/index-futures-stat-arb (daily-bar scaffold, Engle-Granger + OU half-life, no results published) and honoreaa/Futures-MeanReversion (daily, 2021-2025).
- **Entry (objective rules):** 5-minute bars, RTH only. Hedge ratio beta = slope of rolling OLS of ln(NQ) on ln(ES) over the last 20 sessions (re-estimated nightly; the daily-price beta of ES on NQ was 0.2241 in honoreaa, i.e. ~4.46 NQ-points per ES-point). Spread S_t = ln(NQ_t) - beta ln(ES_t). z_t = (S_t - mean(S, 60 bars)) / std(S, 60 bars). Enter when |z| >= 2.0 (QuantConnect template uses 2.33; NexusFi 1.5): z >= +2 -> short NQ / long ES notional-neutral; z <= -2 -> long NQ / short ES. Notional neutrality with micros: 1 MNQ ($2 x NQ) against round(2 NQ / (5 ES)) MES, usually 2 MES per MNQ (spxytrader: "roughly 2 ES contracts short per 1 NQ long"; further scale by the 20-day ATR ratio, NQ ATR ~1.4x ES in percent).
- **Exit:** |z| <= 0.5, or |z| >= 3.5-4.0 (relationship break; QuantConnect uses 4.0), or 18 bars (90 min) time stop, or 15:55 flat. No overnight.
- **Filters:** Trade only if the 20-session ADF test on S rejects a unit root at p <= 0.05 and 20-day daily-return correlation >= 0.90; skip 09:30-09:45 and the 14:00-15:00 FOMC window; off when the correlation-regime gate (#17) says "dispersion" because leadership is changing.
- **Parameters:** beta lookback 20 sessions (10-40); z lookback 60 bars (40-120); entry 2.0 (1.5-2.5); exit 0.5 (0-0.75); stop 3.5 (3-4); time stop 18 bars.
- **Evidence:** honoreaa (daily, 2021-01 to 2025-07, 30-day z): Sharpe 1.67, annualized return 86% at 49% vol, ADF p = 0.039; no cost model. SPY/QQQ pair (PairTradingLab, period unspecified): Sharpe 0.44, 64% win rate, 74 trades, profit factor 1.22; the service closed citing "relationship deterioration, insufficient diversification across single pairs, and multiple-testing bias". QuantConnect ES/NQ templates publish code but no headline statistics. No published intraday ES/NQ pairs backtest with numbers was found in either sweep. Evidence quality **2**.
- **Prop fit:** Hedged exposure keeps daily P&L small and symmetric (good for the 50% consistency rule and the $150 payout-day counter) but doubles commissions and slippage (4 legs per round trip). The killer is a leadership regime change (AI-driven NQ outperformance 2023-2025; April 2025 when NQ beta spiked): spreads trend for days. [FUTURES]: cash SPX/NDX proxies smear the spread by 1-3 minutes of stale prints; 5-minute bars are tolerable, 1-minute bars create fake reversion.
- **Sources:** https://nexusfi.com/a/automation/statistical-arbitrage-futures ; https://github.com/CommunityPokeOrg/index-futures-stat-arb ; https://github.com/honoreaa/Futures-MeanReversion ; https://www.quantconnect.com/learning/articles/investment-strategy-library/intraday-dynamic-pairs-trading-using-correlation-and-cointegration-approach ; https://spxytrader.com/content/intro/nq-vs-es-futures ; https://www.pairtradinglab.com/backtests/YDRpzz59xkkBTt7Y

## 2. NQ-ES 30-minute return-differential fade ("dispersion spike")

- **Origin:** Practitioner variant of #1 on return windows (no cointegration assumption). Standard objective interpretation.
- **Entry:** d_t = cumulative 30-minute log return of NQ minus beta_r x cumulative 30-minute log return of ES, beta_r = 20-session regression slope of 5-min NQ on ES returns (typically 1.2-1.4; stsfutures: NQ daily range 1.5-2x ES in percent). sigma_d = std of d over the same window across the last 60 sessions. Enter when |d_t| >= 2.5 sigma_d: short the outperformer / long the underperformer, notional-neutral.
- **Exit:** d back to 0.5 sigma_d, or 60 minutes, or |d| >= 4 sigma_d, or 15:55.
- **Filters:** Skip 09:30-10:00 (opening dispersion is informative: prop-education sites note NQ tends to lead 09:30-10:30 and ES 10:30-11:30); skip scheduled-release windows (10:00, 14:00).
- **Parameters:** window 30 min (15-60); entry 2.5 sigma (2-3); exit 0.5; stop 4; time stop 60 min.
- **Evidence:** No published backtest. Huth-Abergel and Curme et al. suggest 30-minute dispersion is mostly information, not noise. Evidence quality **1**.
- **Prop fit:** As #1, fewer trades. Research variant only.
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.7103 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0462 ; https://stsfutures.com/learn/nq-vs-es-futures

## 3. NQ/ES ratio-percentile extreme (daily) as an intraday bias

- **Origin:** Practitioner ratio charts (spxytrader, tradealgo, Bookmap). tradealgo: "when the ratio reaches historical extremes (above the 95th percentile of 12-month readings), mean reversion becomes increasingly likely"; spxytrader: "No reliable directional signal exists from the ratio level alone".
- **Rule (objective interpretation):** R = NDX/SPX daily close. If R >= 95th percentile of the trailing 252 sessions and R's 5-day change > 0 -> next session prefer ES over NQ for longs and NQ over ES for shorts (mild "fade tech leadership" bias) and allow the #1 spread short-NQ/long-ES leg at 1.5 z instead of 2.0. If R <= 5th percentile, mirror. Otherwise no effect. Historical range of NQ/ES: 2.8 (2016) to 4.5+ (2021); the ratio is "more volatile during the February, April, July, and October earnings windows".
- **Parameters:** percentile 95/5 (90/10 to 97/3); window 252 days.
- **Evidence:** Descriptive only; no backtest in any source. Evidence quality **1**.
- **Prop fit:** Costless tie-breaker. [DAILY-ONLY].
- **Sources:** https://www.tradealgo.com/trading-guides/futures/index-futures-trading-complete-guide-to-es-nq-ym-and-rty ; https://spxytrader.com/content/intro/nq-vs-es-futures ; https://bookmap.com/blog/nq-vs-es-why-they-move-together-until-they-dont

## 4. Lead-lag "trade the laggard" (NQ move -> ES catch-up, forecast-and-manage)

- **Origin:** Prop-firm folklore ("NQ leads, ES follows"); academic lead-lag literature (Kawaller-Koch-Koch 1987; Chan 1992; Hasbrouck 2003; Huth-Abergel 2011/2014; Curme et al. 2015); "The profitability of lead-lag arbitrage at high frequency" (Int. J. Forecasting 40(3), 2024); aligrithm "Lead-Lag Done Right: Predict and Manage, Don't Naively Wait".
- **Entry (objective):** 1-minute bars. When z(r_NQ, 1-min, 60-bar std) >= 2.0 and ES has not yet moved (r_ES < 0.3 x beta_r x r_NQ), buy/sell ES at the next open. aligrithm's refinement: forecast for the lagger = beta_lag x leader move (e.g. leader +20 bp, beta 0.6 -> +12 bp target); enter immediately, exit when the forecast is reached, time horizon as a backstop only; skip trades whose forecast barely exceeds round-trip costs.
- **Exit:** forecast hit (0.5-1.0 x beta_r |r_NQ|), or 3 minutes, or 1 x ATR(14, 1-min) adverse; flat 15:55.
- **Evidence (negative for our data):** Hasbrouck (2003): "most of the price discovery for both indexes occurs in the E-mini markets"; cross-index lag at minute scale is small. Huth-Abergel: 60% next-midquote accuracy for the lagger at tick scale but "a naive strategy based on market orders cannot make any profit ... because of the bid/ask spread". Int. J. Forecasting 2024: lead-lag arbitrage *is* profitable after costs, latency and execution risk, but on DAX cross-listings with LOB level-1 data (six months of 2013), i.e. sub-second. Cheng et al. (J. Financial Markets 2023): VIX futures lead SPX futures at 15-second intervals, stronger in high volatility and on high-activity days. Mesfin (2026): single-bar MNQ OHLCV edges 0.07-1.50 pts vs 2.0-pt friction. Evidence quality **4 for the phenomenon, 3 (negative) for exploitability at 1-minute**.
- **Prop fit:** Poor. Cost-dominated, unhedged. [FUTURES]: cash-index 1-minute proxies produce a *spurious* "NQ leads SPX" from stale component prints; a backtest on our data will look better than reality. Do not deploy without true futures data.
- **Sources:** https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1540-6261.2003.00609.x ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.7103 ; https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/intfor/v40y2024i3p1002-1021.html ; https://aligrithm.com/lead-lag-done-right-predict-and-manage-dont-naively-wait/ ; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1386418123000496 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04004

## 5. SMT divergence (ICT "Smart Money Technique", ES vs NQ swing failure)

- **Origin:** ICT mentorship; the most popular cross-index signal in the prop-firm community (fundingpips, thesmtdetector, pineify, metrotrade guides; TradingView "RealVardi SMT Divergence NQ vs ES", "SMT Divergence [NQ vs ES]"; r1ckylel Pine code).
- **Entry (codified):** 5-min pivots with strength 3 on both instruments. **Bearish SMT:** after synchronized pivot highs (within +-2 bars), one instrument trades above its pivot while the other does not. Short the instrument that *failed* to make the new high when its next 5-min bar closes back below its own pivot; bullish mirror at lows. Three-index variant (tradealgo): "when two out of three indices fail to make a new high while only one sweeps", the reversal probability "increases significantly" (no number given).
- **Exit:** Stop 1 tick beyond the higher of the two swing highs; target prior swing low or 2R; time stop 60 min; flat 15:55.
- **Filters:** Kill zones 09:30-11:00 and 13:30-15:30; the sweep must take a prior-day high/low, overnight high/low or 09:30-10:00 range; skip FOMC days.
- **Parameters:** pivot strength 3 (2-5); sync tolerance 2 bars; 5-min bars (1-15); R multiple 2 (1.5-3).
- **Evidence:** pineify (2026): "there is no widely cited, independently verified backtested win-rate or academic study quantifying its standalone success rate"; every source describes it as a confirmation layer. 66+ Pine files on GitHub, none with statistics. Evidence quality **1**.
- **Prop fit:** Clean structure (1-3 trades/day, 1R stop, 2R target, flat by 15:55); on trend days both indices keep making highs and the "weak" one catches up -> cap at 2 losses/day. [FUTURES] mild on 5-min pivots.
- **Sources:** https://pineify.app/smt-trading-futures ; https://www.tradingview.com/script/fvmqeLq3-RealVardi-SMT-Divergence-NQ-vs-ES/ ; https://www.tradingview.com/script/QDq5dTaK-SMT-Divergence-NQ-vs-ES/ ; https://github.com/Rickylel/SMT-Divergence-By-r1ckylel ; https://www.tradealgo.com/trading-guides/futures/index-futures-trading-complete-guide-to-es-nq-ym-and-rty

## 6. Opening-range cross-confirmation (ES and NQ both break, or only one breaks)

- **Origin:** SMT-lite on the opening range; standard objective interpretation (no canonical source).
- **Entry:** OR = 09:30-09:45 high/low on both ES and NQ. **Confirmed breakout:** both close a 5-min bar above their OR highs within 5 minutes of each other (by 10:30) -> long the stronger (larger % OR extension). **Divergent breakout:** one closes above its OR high while the other closes back inside -> fade the lone breaker with stop at its high.
- **Exit:** Confirmed: trail 2 x ATR(14, 5-min) or 15:30 exit. Divergent: target OR midpoint, stop 1 tick above high, time 45 min.
- **Filters:** Skip if OR range < 0.3 x ATR(14 days) or > 1.5 x.
- **Evidence:** None published. Mesfin (2026): ORB long at a 75-minute hold on MNQ nets +2.82 pts/trade, T = 0.88, year-unstable; ORB short negative. Cross-confirmation untested. Evidence quality **1**.
- **Prop fit:** As the ORB family; fewer trades.
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04004 ; orb_session.md (this folder)

## 7. Relative-strength instrument selection (ES vs NQ vs GC) for directional day strategies

- **Origin:** Cross-sectional momentum (Jegadeesh-Titman 1993; Asness-Moskowitz-Pedersen 2013, incl. index futures); practitioner rule "trade the strongest/weakest index" (tradealgo: 5-day %-change ranking across ES/NQ/YM/RTY, long the top, short the bottom, 5-10 day hold); Baltussen et al. (2021) per-contract intraday momentum strength; Quantitativo's ES+NQ noise-band portfolio.
- **Rule:** For any long day-strategy, run it in the instrument with the highest RS score; for shorts, the lowest. RS = 0.5 z(overnight return 18:00-09:25 vs 60-day std) + 0.5 z(5-session return). Ratio version: R = NDX/SPX; R > SMA(20) and 5-day change > 0 -> prefer NQ for longs, ES for shorts; else reverse. Gold enters only for longs and only if rho(ES,GC, 20-day) > 0 (gold trading as a risk asset). Default to NQ for last-half-hour momentum (#8): NQ's predictive coefficient and OOS R^2 are the highest among US index futures.
- **Parameters:** weights 0.5/0.5; windows 5 sessions (3-10) and 60 days.
- **Evidence:** AMP 2013: cross-sectional momentum works across index futures (monthly, peer-reviewed). Baltussen Table B1: NQ beta_ROD 6.36 (t 7.97) vs ES 6.18 (t 4.97) vs YM 5.02 (t 4.12). Quantitativo (Databento minute data, 2010-2025, costs $0.85 + $1.40 per contract, 0.25-tick slippage): NQ noise-band intraday momentum 24.3%/yr, Sharpe 1.67, MDD 24%, win 38%, payoff 2.25; ES 16.8%/yr, Sharpe 1.25, MDD 21%; 50/25/25 NQ-strategy/ES-strategy/NQ-long portfolio 22.4%/yr, Sharpe 1.57, MDD 15%, 65% positive months, 2 negative years in 16. stsfutures (3,500 NQ trades, 15 years): the 09:00 ET hour produced 60% of total profit at a 38.4% win rate. No published test of intraday RS as a selector. Evidence quality **4** (monthly CS momentum), **2** (intraday selector).
- **Prop fit:** Costless overlay; feeds #14 sizing because MNQ ($2/pt) and MES ($5/pt) have very different dollar ATRs (NQ range 359 pts = $7,186/contract vs ES 75 pts = $3,735 over the 12 months to May 2025).
- **Sources:** https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12021 ; https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/intramom.pdf ; https://www.quantitativo.com/p/intraday-momentum-for-es-and-nq ; https://stsfutures.com/learn/nq-vs-es-futures ; https://www.tradealgo.com/trading-guides/futures/index-futures-trading-complete-guide-to-es-nq-ym-and-rty

## 8. Cross-asset market intraday momentum (rest-of-day -> last 30 minutes), volatility-conditioned

- **Origin:** Baltussen, Da, Lammers & Martens (2021, JFE 142, 377-403) "Hedging demand and market intraday momentum"; Gao, Han, Li & Zhou (2018, JFE 129) "Market Intraday Momentum"; Xu-Li-Singh-Li (SSRN 4651331) "Cross-Market Intraday Time-Series Momentum" (US last half-hour predicts next-day first half-hour abroad); PMC "Intraday return predictability: commodity ETFs and their volatility indices" (GLD fifth half-hour predicts last half-hour, t = 3.03).
- **Entry (exact, from the paper):** At 15:30, r_ROD = ln(P_15:30 / P_prev-close-16:00). If r_ROD > 0 go long for 15:30-16:00, else short (timing rule eta(r_ROD)). Stronger variant eta(r_ONFH, r_ROD): trade only when the first-half-hour return (prev close to 10:00) and r_ROD have the same sign (Sharpe 1.60, 61% hit rate, fewer trades). For GC, apply the same rule on the COMEX pit-hours window used in the paper (08:20-13:30; last 30 min = 13:00-13:30) or, for our purposes, 13:00-13:30 ET; expect a much weaker effect.
- **Exit:** 16:00 (cash close) flat; no stop in the paper; for Lucid add a stop of 1.0 x ATR(14, 5-min) and never hold past 16:00.
- **Filters:** Gao et al.: the effect is stronger on volatile days, high-volume days and major macro-news days, so require |r_ROD| >= 0.3 x daily ATR(14) (or prior-day VIX >= 18); Baltussen: predictability is strongest when the net gamma exposure of S&P options is negative (not observable for us; VIX > 20 and r_ROD magnitude are reasonable proxies) and the last four months of the sample (Feb-May 2020) were the strongest.
- **Parameters:** cut-off 15:30 (15:00-15:45); magnitude filter 0.3 ATR (0.2-0.5); optional first-half-hour sign agreement.
- **Evidence:** Baltussen Table 6 (equity index futures, 1/N portfolio, Dec 1974-May 2020, gross): eta(r_ROD) 6.86%/yr, 3.96% vol, Sharpe 1.73, success 0.55; eta(r_ONFH, r_ROD) 5.47%, Sharpe 1.60, success 0.61; eta(r_ONFH) 4.21%, Sharpe 1.07; Always Long 0.44%, Sharpe 0.11. Commodities: eta(r_ROD) 4.34%, Sharpe 1.42. "Exploiting the effect in the S&P 500 futures yields a positive net Sharpe ratio when we assume transaction cost equal to a tick." Results similar in 1974-1999 and 2000-2020 subsamples; the effect reverts over the next days (so no overnight carry). Table B1: ES beta_ROD 6.18 (t 4.97, R^2 3.41%, OOS R^2 2.29%); NQ 6.36 (t 7.97, R^2 4.10%, OOS 3.76%); GC beta_ROD 1.09 (R^2 0.25%, OOS 0.08%, weak; silver 2.93 t > 3). Gao et al. (SPY 1993-2013): first-half-hour timing 6.67%/yr at 6.19% vol, Sharpe 1.08 vs 0.29 buy-and-hold. PMC commodity ETF paper: GLD timing Sharpe 5.43 reported (gross, suspiciously high; single study). Evidence quality **5** for ES/NQ, **3** for gold.
- **Prop fit:** One trade a day, 30-minute hold, flat at the cash close, and the 61%-hit-rate sign-agreement variant produces many small positive days. Expected gross edge on NQ: beta 0.0636 x |r_ROD|; with r_ROD = 1% that is ~6 bp = ~15 NQ pts (~$30/MNQ, $300 on 10 MNQ) against a ~1-2 tick cost. The 15:30-16:00 window is where MOC imbalances and gamma hedging land; a 1-ATR stop is essential because the last 30 minutes of Aug 5 2024 / Apr 2025 moved 1%+. [FUTURES] minor (the paper uses futures; cash NDX/SPX 1-min proxies are fine at 30-minute horizons).

## 9. Overnight-intraday (CO-OC) gap reversal conditioned on the VIX change

- **Origin:** Della Corte, Kosowski & Wang (2015) "Market Closure and Short-Term Reversal" (CXO summary): weight assets inversely to their prior close-to-open return and capture the next open-to-close return; "the larger the change in VIX from the prior close to the open, the larger the CO-OC reversal effect for both stocks and, especially, futures". Practitioner gap-fade implementations: TradingView "SP500 Session Gap Fade" (exlux), quantifiedstrategies gap-fill rules (fade 0.1-0.6% gaps, target 75% of the gap), tradealgo "small gaps fill ~65% same session".
- **Entry (objective, time-series version for one instrument):** g = open(09:30) / prior close(16:00) - 1 for ES or NQ. Fade when 0.15% <= |g| <= 0.60% **and** the VIX daily change (prior close to 09:30 print) has the same sign as the gap's "fear" direction (gap down with VIX up >= +5%, gap up with VIX down <= -3%). Enter at 09:35 against the gap. Full-gap version (exlux): today's high below yesterday's low (gap down) or low above yesterday's high (gap up).
- **Exit:** Target 75% of the gap (toward prior close); stop 0.5-1.0 x gap beyond the open (exlux default range 0.5-2.0 x gap); flat 60 min after entry or by 15:30 (exlux: 15-120 min before close).
- **Filters:** No fade when |g| > 0.6% (news gaps continue; Mesfin: gap-continuation short with Kalman velocity z > 2.5 in the first 30 min was the best near-miss, +14.5 pts, T = 1.46); no fade on VIX/VIX3M backwardation days (#11) when gap is down; skip FOMC/CPI mornings.
- **Parameters:** gap band 0.15-0.60%; VIX change +5%/-3%; target 75%; stop 0.75 x gap; hold 60 min.
- **Evidence:** Della Corte et al.: CO-OC in US stocks 1993-2014 gross daily return 1.68%, Sharpe 24.2 (cross-sectional, gross, 100% turnover; most profit "immediately after the open" and vanishes with a 1-minute delay); futures 1982-2014 across index, rate, commodity and FX futures also outperform conventional reversal; the VIX-change conditioning is the paper's key finding for futures. Quantpedia "Short-Term Reversal with Futures" (Wang-Yu 2004-style, weekly, needs volume and open interest) 29.6%/yr, Sharpe 0.82, MDD -58.7% 1983-2000. Mesfin (2026): plain gap-fill fade on MNQ at 09:30/09:45/10:00 was negative (-1.9/-1.1/-1.4 pts net, T ~ -0.3 to -0.4) *without* the VIX condition. exlux script: no statistics beyond "max intraday loss 1.5% on a 100k account" over its last 200 trades. Evidence quality **3** (effect peer-reviewed cross-sectionally; this time-series rule is an interpretation).
- **Prop fit:** One trade in the first hour, defined stop, inside RTH. The VIX conditioning is what separates it from the generic gap fade that Mesfin found below friction. [DAILY-ONLY] for the VIX change (use Yahoo VIX open vs prior close; the 09:30 VIX print is not a clean gap since VIX computes from 03:15).

## 11. VIX term-structure regime gate (VIX/VIX3M, VIX9D/VIX) with "resolution" long days

- **Rule (objective):** r1 = VIX/VIX3M, r2 = VIX9D/VIX from prior-day closes. **Calm contango** r1 < 0.90: trend/continuation on, mean reversion at half size. **Normal** 0.90-1.00: base. **Backwardation** r1 >= 1.00 (or r2 >= 1.05 as early warning: VIX9D crossed above VIX on 29 Jan 2018, a week before Volmageddon): long-only mean reversion and gold shock-day longs on; ORB/trend shorts off; size 0.5x; daily loss cap halved. **Resolution day** (r1 crosses back below 1.00 after >= 1 day above): strongest long-bias day: ORB long, dip buys and #8 long-only at base size for the next 5 sessions.
- **Parameters:** thresholds 0.90/1.00/1.05; look-back for "episode" >= 1 day (Options Cafe) or >= 2 days (prior sweep).

Finaur: trend + VIX regime filter on S&P 500: CAGR 8.4%, MDD -22%, win 58% vs buy-and-hold 9.0%/-55%. Johnson (2017) and Cheng (2019): term-structure slope and VIX premium carry priced information (peer-reviewed; weeks-months). Simon-Campasano/Quantpedia: ~19.7%/yr 2007-2011 in-sample, "slightly negative" OOS. Evidence quality **3** (consistent practitioner statistics through 2026, no peer-reviewed intraday test).

Note the backwardation episodes themselves are net negative for SPY (-0.67%), so "short on backwardation days" is not the trade; "do less, long-only reversion" is. [DAILY-ONLY]; `^VIX3M` and `^VIX9D` are on Yahoo (VIX3M from Sep 2009).

## 13. VIX implied-move channel day trade ("NY VIX Channel Trend")

## 14. Volatility-managed position sizing (ATR / VIX / realized-vol targeting)

Contracts = floor(B / M), capped by Lucid limits (40 micros eval / 20 micros funded) and so that 1.5 x ATR(20) x contracts x point value <= 0.6 x remaining EOD-trailing room. Recompute nightly. Fleming et al.: volatility-timing beats static portfolios with economically large fees. Quantitativo: vol-targeted ES+NQ portfolio MDD 15% vs 21-24% single-market. Evidence quality **5** (monthly); **4** for daily ATR sizing in futures.
- **Prop fit:** The single most important rule for Lucid: constant-dollar-risk days make the 50% consistency test and the five $150 days easy and keep the EOD-trailing floor away. Prevents over-sizing in calm summers and carrying it into August/April spikes.

## 17. Rolling cross-asset correlation-regime filter (ES-NQ, ES-GC)

## 18. Turbulence index / absorption-ratio kill switch (Kritzman)

- **Origin:** Kritzman & Li (2010, FAJ) "Skulls, Financial Turbulence, and Risk Management"; Kritzman, Li, Page & Rigobon (2011, JPM); ORCA (Kriuk 2026, arXiv:2604.17251).
- **Rule:** Daily returns basket y (SPX, NDX, GC, TNX change, DXY, VIX change, plus ETFs). Turbulence d_t = (y_t - mu)' Sigma^-1 (y_t - mu) over a trailing 2-year window. Absorption ratio AR = variance share of the top n/5 eigenvectors over 500 days; shift = (AR_15d - AR_1y)/std(AR_1y). Kill switch: d_t > 90th percentile or AR shift > +1 sigma -> next session: no new directional trades, spreads and gold at half size, daily loss cap halved; resume after 2 non-turbulent days.
- **Parameters:** percentile 90 (85-95); AR window 500 days; shift 1 sigma; basket >= 6.
- **Evidence:** Kritzman & Li: turbulent days persist and have lower returns/higher risk; Kritzman et al.: significant drawdowns preceded by AR spikes (1998-2010, peer-reviewed). ORCA (2026): 24 ETFs, 15 years, 8-fold walk-forward; crash-detection AUC 0.741; rotation CAGR 15.6%, Sharpe 1.13, MDD -7.5% vs buy-and-hold 3.7%/-33.7% (single working paper). Evidence quality **3-4** (risk measure); **3** (overlay).
- **Prop fit:** Pure protection; costs missed trend days after turbulence, a favourable trade for an EOD-trailing account. [DAILY-ONLY].
- **Sources:** https://doi.org/10.2469/faj.v66.n5.3 ; https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2011.37.4.112 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17251

## 20. DXY-gold correlation-gated filter for intraday gold (and DXY trend tie-breaker)

- **Origin:** Murphy "Intermarket Analysis"; Madani (2019, arXiv:1912.12590) intraday gold-USD dependence; Choi (2026, arXiv:2609.03437) gold leads DXY; TradingView "Gold/DXY Correlation Oscillator" (laraibislam): rolling Pearson correlation of gold vs DXY gates RSI signals; practitioner "gold correlation strategy" using DXY and US10Y (fxmbrand, fxnx 90-day rolling betas).
- **Rule (exact, from the script):** corr = Pearson(gold, DXY, 30 bars). Signals only when corr < -0.3 ("inverse regime active"). Buy: RSI(14) crosses above 30; sell: RSI(14) crosses below 70. Any timeframe. **Our implementation:** DXY intraday is not available, so compute corr on daily closes (30 days) and use it as a daily gate for intraday gold mean-reversion (RSI/VWAP) strategies; add the DXY trend tie-breaker: DXY below SMA(20) and 5-day change < 0 -> gold intraday long-only; both above -> short-only or stand aside; |5-day DXY change| > 1% -> 1.25x size in the implied direction.
- **Parameters:** corr window 30 (20-60); threshold -0.3 (-0.2 to -0.5); RSI 14, 30/70.
- **Evidence:** Long-run gold/DXY correlation ~ -0.70 (fazencapital), stronger (< -0.8) in tightening cycles; Madani: negative, significant tail dependence at all intraday scales 2017-2019; Choi (2026): gold is the dominant *transmitter* to FX in a VAR spillover network, so DXY is a poor *leading* filter; 2023-2026: gold $1,800 -> $4,000+ with DXY range-bound/rising (central-bank buying) so the link weakened at daily horizons. No performance claims in the TradingView script ("educational purposes only"). Evidence quality **2**.
- **Prop fit:** Tie-breaker at most; never override price-based signals. [DXY-INTRADAY] not available; [DAILY-ONLY].

## 21. Cross-asset time-series momentum direction filter (daily TSMOM)

- **Origin:** Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen (2012, JFE); Hurst-Ooi-Pedersen (2017); CTA practice.
- **Rule:** Nightly per instrument: TS = sign(C - C_21) + sign(C - C_63) + sign(C - C_252). Intraday breakout/continuation entries only in the TS direction when |TS| >= 2; mean-reversion entries only against TS (buy dips in uptrends). Neutral: base rules.
- **Evidence:** MOP 2012: 58 futures 1985-2009, TSMOM positive in every asset class, Sharpe ~1 diversified; replicated widely; weak 2009-2019, revived 2022. Untested as an intraday gate. Evidence quality **5** (monthly); **2** (intraday gate).
- **Prop fit:** Stops the bot shorting NQ breaks in a 2023-2025 melt-up and buying every dip in 2022. [DAILY-ONLY].

## 22. GMM volatility-regime gated pullback entry (Mesfin "RTH Confluence")

- **Origin:** Mesfin (2026, arXiv:2605.04004) positive controls; HMM/GMM regime literature (Park 2023 arXiv:2307.00459; Blake 2025 arXiv:2510.03236).
- **Entry (from Appendix A, adapted for no-volume data):** 5-min MNQ bars RTH. Features: ATR ratio = ATR(20, 1-min merged to 5-min) / fixed baseline (10.34 pts in the paper); volume z-score (50 bars) -> replace with range z-score [VOL]; close position in bar range; 5-min return. 3-component GMM fit on the training year (0 low activity, 1 active flow, 2 extreme). Signal when regime = 1 AND rolling 200-bar Markov P(1 -> 2) > 0.15 AND volume z > 0.5. Entry = limit at signal-bar close minus 25 x ATR_ratio pts (ATR_ratio capped [0.5, 2.0]), valid 6 bars. Long only.
- **Exit:** Bar 13 after signal (65 min) or end of RTH; stop 80 x ATR_ratio pts (tighten to 40 for Lucid).
- **London variant:** 15-min bars 03:00-08:30, enter long at next open on a clean regime 0 -> 2 transition (no regime 1 in prior 2 bars), exit 60 min or 08:30, stop 20 pts.
- **Evidence:** RTH Confluence: in-sample 2022-2024 N = 538, +15.77 pts/trade net, T = 5.83, 61% win; walk-forward OOS N = 196, +11.82 pts net after 2.0-pt friction, T = 3.11, 2025 OOS +13.14; permutation p < 0.001; unconditional long -2.60 pts. London Signal B: OOS N = 247, +4.09 pts, T = 4.30, win 61.5%; a 1-bar entry delay flips T to -2.78. Author-disclosed: 53+ parameter combinations searched, ATR baseline from full in-sample, one contaminated fold, idealized stop fills. Evidence quality **3**.
- **Prop fit:** 1-2 trades/day, 65-min hold, +11.8 MNQ pts = $23.6/micro; 10 MNQ ~ $236/trade with SD ~$1,076; stop must be tightened and sized by #14. [VOL] (approximate with range z; expect degradation). [FUTURES] mild.
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04004 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.00459 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.03236

## 23. VVG "high gap + high early move" day classifier (late-session fade), negative evidence

- **Origin:** Mesfin (2026b, arXiv:2605.11423) "A Validated Volatility-Volume-Gap Classifier for Regime Identification in MNQ Intraday Data".
- **Rule (exact):** Day is VVG-positive if all three exceed their expanding-window top-tercile thresholds (warm-up 60 days): (1) |first-30-min return|, (2) |overnight gap| = |open_09:30 - prior close| / prior close, (3) first-bar volume z vs 20-day rolling [VOL] (drop or replace with first-bar range z). 40 of 947 days 2022-Aug 2025 (9/5/23/3 by year). Behaviour: gradual drift, peak 14:00-15:30, late reversal; 77.6% of classifier days reverse from their intraday peak before the close (mean give-back 11.7 pts). Best directional rule: fade the opening direction on classifier days only when an OLS regression of first-30-min on last-30-min predicts reversal (beta -0.0416, t = -2.45).
- **Evidence (negative):** Eight directional configurations all failed the author's gates (T >= 2, >= 30 OOS trades, net > 0 after 2-pt friction, year-stable). Best: 127 OOS trades, +7.80 pts net, T = 1.46, 2023 +297.5 / 2024 -26.75 / 2025 +477.25 pts. Mean close on classifier days: 2024 +40.74 pts (persistent drift), 2025 -42.48 (spike and crash). Plain reversal entry N = 289, +1.37 pts, T = 0.86, 51.9% win; continuation N = 1,170, -3.22 pts. Evidence quality **3 (negative)**.
- **Prop fit:** Do not trade as a rule; use the classifier as an "unstable day" flag that halves size and forbids new entries after 14:00. The 2024-vs-2025 sign flip is the clearest published demonstration that gap/vol day-types do not have a stable direction.
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11423 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04004

## 24. VIX open-gap day-type classifier

- **Origin:** Connors "VIX RSI" component (VIX open > prior close); Quantifiable Edges VIX-gap studies (paywalled); "Breaking VIX at open" (JBF 2021: VIX rises overnight on average and falls during trading hours; weekend/holiday overnight rises larger); CO-OC evidence (#9). Objective interpretation.
- **Rule:** VIX_gap = VIX 09:30 print / prior close - 1. **Fear gap** >= +8%: mean-reversion long day (dip buys, VWAP reversion long, gap-down fade per #9) if SPX > SMA(200); no shorts. **Complacency gap** <= -5%: trend-day long bias at base size. Else base rules. If VIX closes below its open on a fear-gap day, hold longs to 15:55 (also feeds #8).
- **Parameters:** +8% (5-12%), -5% (-3 to -8%).
- **Evidence:** Della Corte et al.: larger overnight VIX change -> larger CO-OC reversal, "especially" for futures. "Breaking VIX at open": systematic overnight-up/intraday-down VIX pattern (so the fear gap tends to decay intraday). Simon & Wiggins: contrarian multi-day. No practitioner statistics fetched. Evidence quality **2-3**.
- **Prop fit:** Cheap day-type label; keeps the bot from shorting gap-downs on fear days (V-reversals destroy EOD-trailing accounts). [DAILY-ONLY].
- **Sources:** https://www.cxoadvisory.com/technical-trading/overnightintraday-return-reversal-trading/ ; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378426621000182 ; https://doi.org/10.1002/fut.4

## 25. VIX futures basis / VIX premium / VIX futures lead (signal only; not tradeable with our instruments)

- **Origin:** Simon & Campasano (2014) via Quantpedia "Exploiting Term Structure of VIX Futures"; Cheng (2019, RFS); Cheng et al. (2023, J. Financial Markets) "The lead-lag relation between VIX futures and SPX futures"; PLOS ONE (2024) "VIX constant maturity futures trading strategy: a walk-forward machine learning study"; QuantConnect forum replication ("Trading strategies based on VIX term structure").
- **Original rule:** Short (long) the front VIX future with >= 10 trading days to maturity when the daily roll [(front - VIX)/days] > +0.10 (< -0.10), hold 5 trading days, hedged with E-mini S&P by regression. **For us:** only the sign is usable: daily roll > +0.10 = carry regime (equity-friendly, allow trend longs); < -0.10 = stress regime (same actions as backwardation in #11). Approximate the basis with (VIX3M - VIX)/63 since `VX=F` is sparse on Yahoo.

Cheng et al. (2023): VIX futures lead SPX futures at 15-second horizons, stronger under high volatility and on high cross-market activity days (not exploitable at 1-minute without VIX futures ticks). Evidence quality **3** for the vol-product strategy; **2** for the equity gate.
- **Prop fit:** Signal only. [VIXF] [VIX-INTRADAY] [DAILY-ONLY].

## What the evidence says works in 2022-2026

1. **Last-30-minute cross-asset momentum is the family's only entry signal with 5-grade evidence (#8).** Baltussen et al. show it in 1974-1999 and 2000-2020 alike, strongest in Feb-May 2020, with NQ the best single contract (OOS R^2 3.8%) and a positive net Sharpe in ES at one-tick costs. The mechanism (short-gamma hedging by option market makers and leveraged ETFs, plus MOC flow) grew through 2024-2026 with 0DTE and leveraged-ETF volumes, which is the right direction for persistence; the risk is the occasional 1%+ last-half-hour reversal on crash days, so a stop and #11/#12 gating are mandatory. Build it first, in NQ, with the sign-agreement variant (61% hit rate) for consistency.
2. **VIX term-structure gating has held through 2026 (#11).** Both independent trackers (43 and 103 episodes) show the same shape: backwardation episodes are short (median 2 days), net negative while they last, and followed by strong, high-hit-rate longs once VIX/VIX3M crosses back below 1.0 (+3.0% in 5 days, 88%). The Connors 5%-stretch rule is *not* supported (CXO), so use the term structure, not the VIX vs its 10-day SMA, as the regime gate.
3. **Sizing and off-switches beat signals (#14, #11, #12, #18, #13).** Volatility-managed sizing is 5-grade evidence; the VIX implied-move channel (#13) is a neat way to express stops in VIX units. For a $2,000 EOD-trailing floor checked intraday, the practical build is: base strategies sized by ATR/VIX, halved or off on backwardation/turbulence days, long-biased on resolution days and fear-gap days (#24), instrument chosen by relative strength (#7), direction gated by daily TSMOM (#21).
4. **ES/NQ relative-value is weak in this era (#1-#5).** Correlation reached 0.97-0.99 in 2022 and 2025 and ~0.95 intraday, so ES carries little information NQ does not; lead-lag is sub-minute and spread-bound; SMT has zero statistics; the one spread backtest is daily, single-source and from a period of repeated leadership breaks (2023-2025 AI rally, April 2025). Test the spread only hedged, with a 3.5-z stop and the #17 gate; expect it to earn mostly in the calm-dispersion regimes (2017-type years), not in 2022/2025.
5. **Overnight-gap reversal needs the VIX condition (#9).** Unconditional gap fades were below friction on MNQ 2022-2025 (Mesfin); the peer-reviewed CO-OC effect is largest when the overnight VIX change is large, especially in futures. Fade only the small-to-medium gaps on fear-gap mornings.
6. **Gold is a risk asset now, not a hedge (#19, #20, #17).** 5-minute GC/ES correlation +0.17, 1-month correlations up to +0.95, gold sold with stocks on the April 2025 margin cascade, and a 2025 journal paper documenting the fading safe-haven effect. Keep the shock-day long as a rare specialist trade with the liquidity-cascade exclusion; keep DXY as a tie-breaker; never size gold as a hedge for an index position.
7. **Regime-gated, 60-75 minute pullback holds are the surviving intraday architecture (#22), and gap/vol day-types have no stable direction (#23).** The 2024 (+40.7 pts drift) vs 2025 (-42.5 pts crash) flip on identical classifier days is the clearest warning in this sweep: classify the day to decide *size and exposure window*, not direction.
8. **Lucid-specific build order.** (i) #14 sizing on everything; (ii) #8 NQ last-30-minute momentum with sign agreement and a 1-ATR stop; (iii) #11 + #12 + #24 as daily gates; (iv) #9 VIX-conditioned gap fade and #22 regime pullbacks as the second and third trade generators; (v) #19 shock-day gold as the crash-day specialist; (vi) #1 spread and #5 SMT only if the backtest shows a positive edge net of 2 ticks per leg, which the literature does not promise.
